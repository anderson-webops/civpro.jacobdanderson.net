Jurisdiction and Removal

Core question: Which court has power, and what changes the forum without necessarily ending the case?

Cases: Repair Shop Removal Trap, Interstate Tire Failure, Viral Defamation Stream.

Instructor prompts: Require separate personal-jurisdiction and subject-matter-jurisdiction explanations. When removal appears, ask for the original-jurisdiction basis and the forum-defendant analysis.

PackSuggested sequenceDebrief target
30One round; force either removal/remand or a personal-jurisdiction contrast.Forum change versus dismissal.
50Two rounds with role rotation; compare a forum defendant with an out-of-state defendant.Complete diversity and removal availability.
75Three rounds; require a different forum analysis in each.Transfer across fact patterns, not memorized labels.

Rule 12 Motion Practice

Core question: Which threshold defense fits, when must it be raised, and is the right response opposition, cure, transfer, or amendment?

Cases: Broken SaaS Rollout, Screening Report Pleading Fight, Bank Data Breach.

Instructor prompts: Ask whether each defense is waivable before it is played. Require the plaintiff to classify the response as defeating, curing, or redirecting the attack.

PackSuggested sequenceDebrief target
30One round centered on service or pleading sufficiency.Curable defects and responsive cards.
50Two rounds; make the second defense confront serial-motion waiver.Rule 12(g)/(h) timing.
75Three rounds; compare service, pleading, and jurisdiction attacks.Why similarly named motions have different consequences.

Discovery and Rule 56

Core question: Which discovery device produces the needed proof, and what happens when the record remains incomplete?

Cases: Interstate Tire Failure, Viral Defamation Stream, Platform Wage Claim.

Instructor prompts: Make students name the proof item before choosing a tool. Close at least one round early so Rule 56 exposes the missing record.

PackSuggested sequenceDebrief target
30One round; collect one item, mishandle or skip another, then close discovery.Tool-to-proof matching.
50First round reaches trial; second closes with a missing item.Complete versus incomplete record.
75Three rounds featuring burden, privacy, and privilege responses.Proportionality and objection-specific moves.

Joinder and Supplemental Jurisdiction

Core question: Does the party or claim share the required factual relationship, and what happens to jurisdiction after it is added?

Cases: Platform Wage Claim, Whistleblower Plus Rent Dispute, Interstate Tire Failure.

Instructor prompts: Require a same-transaction fact for joinder and a common-nucleus fact for supplemental jurisdiction. Recalculate citizenship and court after every joinder result.

PackSuggested sequenceDebrief target
30One round contrasting related and unrelated claims.Common nucleus.
50Two rounds; one claim joinder and one party joinder.Different relationship tests and consequences.
75Three rounds with removal consequences after a local party is added.Post-joinder jurisdiction math.

Erie and Class Actions Preview

Core question: Is the disputed rule substantive or procedural, and what threshold fit must a proposed class satisfy?

Cases: Bank Data Breach, Auto-Renewal Class Preview, Screening Report Pleading Fight.

Instructor prompts: Label Erie conclusions as provisional and require a Rule 23 reason rather than accepting the class label. Keep subject-matter jurisdiction distinct from both preview issues.

PackSuggested sequenceDebrief target
30One round focused on either Erie or Rule 23.Issue separation.
50Two rounds, one Erie and one class-certification preview.Substantive/procedural and Rule 23 fit.
75Three rounds adding a pleading or SMJ contrast.Avoid collapsing distinct threshold doctrines.

Common final debrief

  1. Which card choice had the strongest fact-to-rule explanation?
  2. Which wrong motion or missing proof item appears in the assessment?
  3. Did the result end the case, change the forum, cure a defect, or only narrow the dispute?
  4. What would change under fuller doctrine beyond this model?
  5. What one balance or wording change should be tested with the same pack and seed?
